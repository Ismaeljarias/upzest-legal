2. Description of Service

Upzest is a Shopify application that enables merchants to display upsell offers to customers after they add a product to their cart. Features include the ability to configure upsell products, optional discount rules, and modal customization.

The App is provided through the Shopify App Store and operates within the permissions granted by Shopify's Partner Program.

3. Plans, Billing, and Downgrade Policy

Free Plan: The Free plan is available at no cost and allows you to create, edit, and maintain up to 3 active upsell offers. Customizing the upsell modal appearance is not available on the Free plan.

Pro Plan: The Pro plan is available for $19.99 USD per month, billed through Shopify's billing system. The Pro plan includes unlimited active offers and full modal customization. A 7-day free trial is available for new Pro subscribers.

Downgrade and Offer Deactivation: If your plan is downgraded from Pro to Free — whether by canceling your Pro subscription or due to a failed payment — the App will automatically deactivate any active offers exceeding the Free plan limit of 3. The 3 most recently created active offers will remain active; all others will be paused. Deactivated offers are not deleted and can be reactivated by upgrading to Pro again.

Billing through Shopify: All payments are processed by Shopify. We do not store or handle your payment information directly. Subscription management, cancellations, and refund requests are subject to Shopify's billing policies.
